# UGC Roadmap style

His ask (2026-11-24): everything made for UGC Roadmap - guides, PDFs, decks, pages for his students - has its
own look, the logo's, not the default one. Use it every time without being asked.

- **Colours** (from the logo, `public/ugc-roadmap-512.png`): page cream `#F4F3EF`, ink near-black `#0B0C0F`,
  soft text `#5E5D58`, cards white `#FFFFFF`, thin lines `#D9D7D0`. No other colour - no blue, green or orange.
- **Logo**: top left of the first page, with "UGC ROADMAP" in bold capitals beside it and a line under it
  (e.g. "The Cutter · student guide"); small again in every page's footer, before "UGC ROADMAP · <title>" and
  the page number.
- **The road**: the logo's road as the divider - a black rounded band with white dashes down its middle - under
  the title and between big sections.
- **Steps**: "STEP n" in a small black pill with cream text, then the step's name in bold.
- **Boxes**: "Important" = black box, cream text. "Check it worked" = white box with a thin black border.
  Labels in small bold capitals.
- **Tables**: black header row with cream text, white rows, thin black border.
- **Type**: one plain sans (DejaVu Sans in PDFs made here), bold for buttons and names on screen; addresses,
  branches and paths in mono on a white chip.
- **Words**: plain, short, for students: what to tap, what they should see, what to do if not.

Example: the Railway server guide for his students (Cutter-server-on-Railway.pdf, 2026-11-24).
